Outfit Maker - Cloth AI Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

Introduction

Outfit Maker - Cloth AI (“the app”) is an AI stylist from GlintTec Apps (GenieMagic Labs Pvt Ltd), “we” or “us”. It helps you find your body shape and, as new features arrive, get outfit ideas that suit you. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. There is no account or login: you never give us your name, email address or phone number to use the app.

Information We Collect

  • Photos: a photo you take with the camera or choose from your gallery for the body shape scan.

  • Profile answers and results: your gender and age range (if you answer them), the body shape you pick or that the AI finds, and the analysis result.

  • App settings: your app language and whether you finished the intro screens.

  • An anonymous ID: the app signs you in anonymously. This gives you a random ID that is not linked to your name or contact details. We use it to keep the service secure and to find errors.

  • Purchase information: when you buy credits, Google Play and our billing provider (RevenueCat) record the purchase against an anonymous ID. We never see your card or payment details.

  • Technical and usage data: app version, Android version, device model, language, crash reports, error diagnostics and how you use the app’s features (for example which screens you open).

  • Advertising data: if ads are shown and you have allowed it where required, our advertising partners may use your device’s advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and ad interactions.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you, when you take or choose a photo, or answer a question in the app.

  • Automatically, from the app and the services it uses, for security, diagnostics, analytics and ads.

How We Use Information

  • To analyze your photo and show you your body shape and styling results.

  • To remember your profile on your phone so you do not have to enter it again.

  • To process purchases and add credits.

  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the app, and to prevent abuse and fraud.

  • To show ads, where ads are shown, and measure how they perform.

  • To comply with legal obligations when required.

Photos and AI Processing

The body shape scan needs cloud-based AI. When you ask the app to analyze a photo:

  • On your phone, the photo is resized and re-saved, and hidden data inside it (such as location and camera details) is removed.

  • The photo is sent over a secure connection to our servers (Google Firebase Cloud Functions), together with your app language so the answer is written in your language.

  • Our servers pass the photo to OpenAI, our third-party AI provider, which analyzes it and returns the result to us and then to you.

  • We do not save your photo on our servers or keep a copy after your request is finished. Our server logs hold only technical information (your anonymous ID, language, whether it worked, how long it took, and error details) and never the photo itself.

OpenAI processes the photo under its own terms and privacy policy (linked below). Only use the scan if you are comfortable with your photo being processed this way. If you are not, use the manual body shape options, which do not send a photo anywhere.

We may add more AI styling features, such as outfit suggestions, virtual try-on and face swap. These may also send your photo, or text you type, to an AI provider to produce the result you asked for. We will update this policy before such a feature is released.

Data Stored on Your Phone

  • Your photo, profile answers, analysis result and settings are saved only on your phone.

  • Your credit balance is also saved on your phone. It is not kept in an account, so deleting the app’s data or uninstalling the app can remove it, and we cannot restore it.

  • You can delete your profile and saved photo at any time from Settings > Profile Details > Clear Data. Uninstalling the app removes everything saved on your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: asked only when you choose to take a photo.

  • Photos: the app uses the system photo picker, so it sees only the photo you choose and never your whole gallery.

  • Saving to your gallery: only when you choose to save a result.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app (listed below): OpenAI for AI analysis, Google and Firebase for hosting, security, analytics and crash reporting, Google Play and RevenueCat for purchases, and Google AdMob with AppLovin for ads. We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the app.

Data Retention

  • Photos: we do not keep them on our servers (see Photos and AI Processing). Photos and profile data on your phone stay until you clear them or uninstall the app.

  • Server logs: limited technical logs are kept for a short period (generally about 30 days) for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting, and then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

  • Analytics, crash and purchase records are kept by Google, RevenueCat and our other providers for the periods set in their own policies.

Advertising and Your Choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent to ads personalization before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy Choices, when that option is shown.

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings.

  • You choose whether to use AI features. If you do not want a photo sent to an AI provider, do not use the photo scan.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, and to have it corrected, deleted or restricted, and to object to some processing. Because the app has no account, almost everything is on your phone and under your control. For any request about technical data linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International Processing

Our providers (such as Google and OpenAI) may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. We rely on these providers to protect data as described in their own policies.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted connections and app attestation checks, to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. For significant changes, we will update this page before the change takes effect.
